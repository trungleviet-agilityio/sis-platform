# ADR rules

- Skeleton: title `# ADR NNNN: <decision>`, Status, Date, Context, Decision (numbered tables), Alternatives
  considered, Consequences (with the owner's checkpoint commands), Well-Architected review, Open questions,
  Sources.
- Mark every external fact (V) official text seen, (S) secondary source, (C) confirm at a checkpoint, and cite the
  URL.
- The Well-Architected review covers all six pillars with best practice IDs, how the design meets each, and the
  gap with the phase that closes it.
- Each decision with real choices has an options table with pros and cons. Accepted risks say "accepted".
- An accepted ADR changes only through a dated amendment section or a new ADR that supersedes it.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes; plain English.
